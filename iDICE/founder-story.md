# Founder Story

I graduated from Madonna University and witnessed the chaos of paper-based examinations firsthand. But the problem didn't end there—I have friends from UNIBEN and UNN who are still experiencing it.

In 2026, hundreds of students at UNN faced a crisis: missing scripts, mixed-up registration/matriculation numbers, and slow result updates. Even now, some students still haven't found their exam papers.

This isn't negligence—it's the inevitable result of overwhelming bulk. Papers get lost in transit to offices. They get mixed up during marking. With thousands of scripts to process, you can't blame the lecturers. The workload is simply too much for any human to handle without error.

I'm building Ambezi to fix this once and for all. Not just for one university, but for all 463 tertiary institutions in Nigeria. No more missing scripts. No more delayed graduations. No more students losing years because their exam results are "missing."

I've lived this problem. I've seen the pain it causes. And I'm building the solution.
