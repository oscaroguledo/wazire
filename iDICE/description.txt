# 🎯 1. What You’re Building (Core Idea)

A **centralized digital examination system** that supports:

* Universities (e.g., University of Nigeria, Nsukka, University of Benin)
* Exam bodies like West African Examinations Council, Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board, National Examinations Council

---

# 🧱 2. Core System Modules

## **A. Authentication & Roles (CRITICAL)**

Users must log in with roles:

* Student
* Lecturer / Examiner
* School Admin
* Super Admin

👉 Use **RBAC (Role-Based Access Control)**

---

## **B. Exam Creation System**

* Question types:

  * MCQ
  * Essay
  * File upload
* Time limits
* Randomization of questions
* Question banks

---

## **C. Exam Delivery (Anti-Cheating Focus)**

### 🔒 Required Features:

* Webcam monitoring (face detection)
* Microphone monitoring (noise detection)
* Screen monitoring:

  * Detect tab switching
  * Detect screen recording
* Fullscreen enforcement
* AI flagging suspicious behavior

⚠️ Important:
You are entering **proctoring territory**—this is complex and sensitive.

---

## **D. Marking System**

* Auto-mark MCQs instantly
* Manual marking interface for essays
* Multiple markers (moderation system)
* Anonymous marking (no student names)

---

## **E. Result Management**

* Instant result generation
* No “missing scripts” → all results stored digitally
* Grade computation
* Export to PDF / school systems(using external apis or csv or excel)

---

## **F. Audit & Integrity System (VERY IMPORTANT)**

* Log everything:

  * Who created exam
  * Who edited
  * Who marked
  etc
* Immutable logs (cannot be changed)

👉 This is how you eliminate “missing results”

---

## **G. Admin Dashboard**

* Monitor all exams
* Detect anomalies
* Manage institutions

---

## **Cloud**

# ⚠️ 4. Hard Truths (Don’t Skip This)

## ❌ You CANNOT guarantee “no cheating”

Even big systems struggle with this.

👉 What you can do:

* **Reduce cheating significantly**
* **Detect suspicious behavior**

---

## ⚠️ Legal & Privacy Issues

* Camera + microphone monitoring = **privacy sensitive**
* You MUST:

  * Get user consent
  * Follow data protection laws

---

## ⚠️ Infrastructure Challenge

For places like WAEC/JAMB:

* Millions of students
* Requires:

  * Massive servers
  * Offline fallback options

---

# 🧠 5. Smarter Strategy (Start Small)

* One university (e.g., UNN)

Add proctoring later
